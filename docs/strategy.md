# Product strategy and roadmap

3PM is testing a direct-to-fan model with its own artists and releases. The public product combines music discovery, direct purchases, creative services and membership. Artist ownership and non-exclusive participation guide the model.

| Audience | What 3PM offers | Public reference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Artists | Release music, reach listeners directly and offer creative services while retaining ownership. | [For artists](https://3rdpersonmusic.com/for-artists/) |
| Fans | Discover music, support artists directly and explore members-only vault content. | [For fans](https://3rdpersonmusic.com/for-fans/) |
| Creative clients | Find producers, songwriters, engineers and featured artists with visible service scope and pricing. | [Service market](https://3rdpersonmusic.com/service-market/) |
| Investors | Follow the development of the platform and its business model across releases, services and membership. | [For investors](https://3rdpersonmusic.com/for-investors/) |

## Planning priorities

The following is a working synthesis of the public product direction. Dates and commitments belong in individual project decisions.

1. **Prove the artist and listener experience.** Make it easy to go from an artist page to listening, buying a release or discovering a service. Use the existing catalogue to find and fix friction.
2. **Improve repeat participation.** Develop the journey from an initial purchase to repeat support through memberships, vault content and rewards. The [fan page](https://3rdpersonmusic.com/for-fans/) describes cashback on completed orders paid with $3PM.
3. **Make creative services easier to buy.** Give prospective clients enough information to understand deliverables, pricing and how to start a project.
4. **Document what can be reused.** Record the custom components, their dependencies and the checks needed to reproduce behavior. Use the repository to share the resulting blueprint.

Useful measures for evaluating these priorities include release purchase completion, repeat support, member retention and completed service bookings. These are proposed measures.
