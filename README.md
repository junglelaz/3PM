# 3PM · 3rd Person Music

[3rd Person Music](https://3rdpersonmusic.com/) is an independent music platform built by musicians. It brings releases, artist profiles, creative services, memberships, and direct fan support together. 3PM uses its own artists and releases to test the model before sharing what works more widely.

## Explore 3PM

- [For artists](https://3rdpersonmusic.com/for-artists/) — artist ownership, non-exclusive participation, and ways to earn through music and creative work.
- [For fans](https://3rdpersonmusic.com/for-fans/) — discover and support artists, explore vault memberships, and see how $3PM rewards work on eligible orders.
- [Service market](https://3rdpersonmusic.com/service-market/) — find songwriting, production, engineering, and featured-artist services.
- [For investors](https://3rdpersonmusic.com/for-investors/) — the business model, product direction, and work ahead.
