# 3PM · 3rd Person Music

[3rd Person Music](https://3rdpersonmusic.com/) is an independent music platform built by musicians. It combines releases, artist profiles, creative services, memberships, and direct fan support. 3PM uses its own artists and releases to test the model.

## Explore 3PM

- [For artists](https://3rdpersonmusic.com/for-artists/) — artist ownership, non-exclusive participation, and ways to earn through music and creative work.
- [For fans](https://3rdpersonmusic.com/for-fans/) — discover and support artists, explore vault memberships, and see how rewards work.
- [Service market](https://3rdpersonmusic.com/service-market/) — songwriting, production, engineering, and featured-artist services.
- [For investors](https://3rdpersonmusic.com/for-investors/) — the business model, product direction, and work ahead.
